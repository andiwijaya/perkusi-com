# Local canonical media archive

B1 copied **18 protected media files /17,410,563 bytes** and **39 source, license and recipe files**. Originals were neither moved nor deleted. Exact original-to-canonical paths, sizes and SHA256 are recorded in `ARCHIVE_MANIFEST.json`. The central `MEDIA_INVENTORY.csv` has679 rows, including622 existing internal files and57 copied files; existing large libraries were not duplicated. Generated inventory/README/archive-manifest files are excluded from their own row count.

## Suling

`suling/source-packet/` preserves the original WebM, native decoded Float32 PCM/WAV, primary source and complete license page, intake/transform records, and source recipes. `suling/technical-packet/` preserves three raw diagnostic excerpts, attribution, the full readable CC BY-SA4.0 license, exact extraction/quality metadata and analysis recipes.

Suling remains **NOT PRODUCTION QUALIFIED**. It is not a curated playable bank or a new Central Javanese CdM/equal-tempered patch. Original CC BY-SA4.0 notices and modification records are retained. Future adaptations/exports need the license treatment appropriate to their actual content; this archive grants no blanket license to all future exports or application code.

## Beatbox masters

`beatbox-masters/masters-48k/` preserves twelve authored48kHz masters. `recipe/scripts/make-original-groove.py` and `provenance/` preserve the original recipe, score/manifest, generation proof and CC0 notices. The historical recipe keeps its original workspace-relative paths; review those paths before any future execution. Live32kHz delivery under `internal/original-groove/audio/` is unchanged.

## Preservation and publication

Historical packet MANIFEST/SEAL copies describe their original packets. Rejected-source pages, browser profiles and unrelated test logs were not copied. The new archive manifest identifies the exact selected subset and mappings; copied reports may retain old workspace paths/build identities. Selected source-packet relative links are preserved. Original-to-archive mappings must accompany any later approved removal of QA originals.

Archive payload directories are ignored by Git. Only this README, the central CSV and archive manifest are whitelisted as small metadata. No asset is registered/imported into the application. Current Vite globs, public directory and delivery-plugin selections exclude this archive; fresh emitted-output qualification belongs to a later build gate.

This is a local preservation copy, not an off-device backup or deployed library. B1 made no runtime/recovery changes, user-project changes, deletion, mic/listening claim or audio-engine change. Future feature work requires its own approved slice.
