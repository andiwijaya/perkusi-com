Source acquisition must unwind its own partial allocation when rejecting before returning a lease. The coordinator owns only its specific input edge, one replay gain route, abort/generation, real CaptureSession and its retained take pins. Mixed master input is rejected. Arbitrary instrument/choke/FX/mic/sparse buses are outside this contract.
